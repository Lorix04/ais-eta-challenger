# M1X Augusta research geometry

## Semantics

The M1X target is a **versioned research-gate entrance event** into Porto Megarese. It is not represented as an official ATA, Pilot Boarding Place, port-limit event, berth arrival or all-fast timestamp.

Augusta requires a two-entrance representation. The Autorità di Sistema Portuale describes the port as outer roadstead plus inner roadstead/Porto Megarese, protected by roughly 6.5 km of breakwaters with two openings. The Istituto Idrografico Portolano identifies the openings as **Levante** and **Scirocco** and documents different operational rules for the two passages.

## Levante gate

The research cross-section uses rounded Istituto Idrografico light-list positions for the illuminated marks near the north end of Diga Centrale and Diga Settentrionale. This makes the line domain-anchored, but it remains a research target rather than a legal boundary.

## Scirocco gate

The available official text establishes the Scirocco passage and its operational constraints, but the current project does not have a verified official centre-line coordinate pair suitable for a precise cross-section. The frozen Scirocco segment is therefore an explicit **research proxy**, anchored to official breakwater reference coordinates. Any retained Scirocco event is capped at confidence B until authoritative geometry is obtained.

## Inner Porto Megarese proxy

The inner polygon is used only as an analysis envelope to confirm port-side stopping after a crossing. It is not a legal port polygon.
